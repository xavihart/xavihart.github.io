About

I work on generative AI and Physical AI. My research spans video diffusion models and their post-training, world models and world action models for robotics, multimodal large language models, and the safety of generative models. I received my B.E. in Computer Science (Zhiyuan Honors Program) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2022.

Skills: diffusion models; post-training of diffusion models; world models; world action models; multimodal LLMs.
